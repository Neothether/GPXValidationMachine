The CDM Query Bot is used by clinical data managers on a Phase III hypertension trial. It reviews
blood-pressure readings entered by investigator sites into the trial database, identifies readings that
indicate a possible hypertensive crisis, and drafts data queries that are sent to site investigators.
Missed readings could delay recognition of a serious adverse event. The readings and resolved queries
form part of the clinical data set submitted to FDA and EMA, so the tool creates and changes GxP records
and must meet 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11 expectations for access control, audit trail and data export.
